## Review

Every generated image was visually inspected. All depict one complete, connected quill with a writing nib toward the lower left, feather toward the upper right, a clear grip, and distinct rarity ornamentation. There is no lettering, interface, border, scenery, or cropped edge. Metadata checks confirmed square PNGs with alpha. The generator used tighter transparent margins than the requested 10% (roughly 3–6% at the plume tip), so consumers should use contained sizing and their own surrounding layout space. The written spec's nib/tip coordinates are approximate artistic targets rather than exact pixel anchors.
